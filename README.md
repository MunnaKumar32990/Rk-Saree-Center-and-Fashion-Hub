# 🛍️ RK Saree & Fashion Hub

A complete online shopping platform for **RK Saree Center** — allowing customers to browse, shop, and order sarees and clothing from the comfort of their home.

🌐 **Shop Online:** [https://rk-saree-center-and-fashion-hub.vercel.app](https://rk-saree-center-and-fashion-hub.vercel.app)

---

## 🛒 What Customers Can Do

- **Browse & Search** — Find products by category, subcategory, color, size, price, and rating
- **Product Details** — View multiple images, size guide, colors, and reviews
- **Shopping Cart** — Add items, apply coupons, and see your total in real time
- **Wishlist** — Save items for later
- **Place Orders** — Choose Cash on Delivery or online payment via Razorpay
- **Track Orders** — View real-time order status from confirmed to delivered
- **Cancel Orders** — Cancel before dispatch from your order history
- **Returns** — Request a return on delivered orders
- **Download Invoice** — Get a PDF invoice for any order
- **Your Profile** — Manage your name, address, and password
- **Notifications** — Get notified when an out-of-stock item is back
- **Share Products** — Share products with friends via WhatsApp or other apps

---

## 🛠️ Admin Capabilities

- **Dashboard** — View daily and monthly revenue, total orders, and key stats
- **Product Management** — Add, edit, or remove products with images, sizes, colors, and discounts
- **Order Management** — Update order status, add tracking numbers, mark Cash on Delivery as delivered
- **Bulk Updates** — Update multiple orders at once
- **User Management** — View customer accounts, suspend or ban accounts if needed
- **Returns & Refunds** — Review and process return requests
- **Coupons** — Create discount codes for customers
- **Announcements** — Display promotional banners across the store
- **Export Orders** — Download order history as a CSV file for accounting
